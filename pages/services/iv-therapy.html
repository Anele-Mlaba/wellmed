FAQ

Things people often ask.

Is IV therapy safe?

When prescribed and administered by a doctor, yes — IV vitamin therapy has decades of clinical use. We screen every patient for allergies, kidney function and contraindications before your first drip.

How often should I drip?

For maintenance, most people do monthly. For acute needs (recovery, illness, training peaks), a short course of weekly drips is common. We'll recommend what suits you.

Will I feel something during?

A brief pinch when the cannula goes in, then nothing. Some people feel a slight warmth or a metallic taste — both normal and short-lived.

Do you take medical aid for IV?

IV therapy is generally a private/wellness service. Some elements may be claimable depending on your medical aid plan — we'll help you submit if applicable.
