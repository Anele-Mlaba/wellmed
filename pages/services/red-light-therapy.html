How it works

The kindest reset your mitochondria will get this week.

Specific wavelengths of red (~660nm) and near-infrared (~850nm) light are absorbed by the cytochrome c oxidase enzyme inside your mitochondria — gently nudging cellular ATP production, reducing oxidative stress and supporting tissue repair.

In plain English: your cells make energy more efficiently, inflammation calms down a notch, and a lot of small things tend to get a little better.
