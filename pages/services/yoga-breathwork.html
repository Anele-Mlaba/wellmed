A studio that fits the practice

Where the prescription becomes a practice.

As a doctor, Dr Moodley kept "prescribing" yoga and breathwork to patients — and watching them not quite get there. So she built the studio next to the consulting room. Not to compete with the city's other lovely teachers, but to offer continuity: a soft, supported entry point into the practices we already know help.

Classes are small. Pace is gentle. Beginners are welcome — actively, warmly, every single time.

FAQ

First-time questions.

I'm not flexible. Is this for me?

Especially for you. Flexibility is what yoga gives you, not what it asks of you. Mention you're new at booking and you'll be looked after.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and an open mind. Mats, props and blankets are provided. Water always welcome.

I have a medical issue — can I still attend?

Probably yes — and Dr Moodley is right here. Tell us at booking and we'll guide you to the right class, or offer a private session first if needed.

Do you offer private classes?

Yes. 1:1 sessions in the studio or in your home, including breathwork-only sessions for stress and sleep work.
