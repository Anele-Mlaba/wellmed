What it is

A short biology lesson, kindly.

Ozone (O₃) is oxygen with an extra atom and that extra atom turns out to be biologically useful in carefully measured medical doses. It's been studied and practised in European medicine since the 1930s, and is used today as an adjunct to support oxygenation, immune balance and the body's antioxidant systems.

At WellMed, ozone is never a stand-alone "cure" it's one tool in a doctor-led plan, used at clinically appropriate concentrations, only after a careful screening consultation.

Major Autohemotherapy (MAH)

A small volume of your blood is drawn, gently mixed with medical ozone and reinfused. The classical protocol.

Insufflations

Rectal or aural ozone insufflations a non-invasive option commonly used in long-term wellness courses.

Topical & Bagging

Localised ozone application for wounds, joints and skin issues useful as an adjunct to other therapies.
