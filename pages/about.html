Meet Dr Moodley

Fifteen years a GP. A lifetime a healer.

Born and raised in KwaZulu-Natal, Dr Moodley always knew she wanted to be a doctor — but it was the spaces between the appointments, the moments where patients quietly asked "what else can I do?", that shaped the practice she now leads.

After completing her MBChB and several years in busy public-sector and private GP practice, she watched the same patterns play out across her panel: stressed bodies, depleted nervous systems, conditions that the textbook treated but didn't truly heal. So she went back to study — adding integrative medicine, IV nutrient therapy, ozone protocols and a 200-hour yoga teacher training to her toolkit.

WellMed is the result: a practice where she doesn't have to choose between evidence-based GP care and the holistic therapies her patients were already seeking elsewhere.

A Career, Briefly

What 15+ years of medicine looks like, told briefly.

2010 – 2014

Public Sector GP

Worked across busy public clinics and casualty wards in Durban, where she learned the breadth of community medicine — from acute trauma to long-tail chronic disease.

2014 – 2019

Private GP Associate

Joined a respected private group practice and built a loyal panel of families. Her patients kept asking for "more than the prescription" — which planted the seed for everything that followed.

2017 – ongoing

Integrative Medicine Training

Began her parallel education in functional and integrative medicine, IV therapy and lifestyle medicine — formalising what she'd been quietly practising for years.

2022 – Today

Founder, WellMed Wellness

Opened WellMed in Umhlanga as a single home for everything she'd trained in — GP medicine, integrative therapies, breathwork, yoga and an unhurried way of caring.

Why She Does This

"Healing isn't loud.
It's the slow art of being properly seen."

Ask Dr Moodley what keeps her in medicine after fifteen years and she'll tell you about a patient — almost always a patient. The mother who finally slept after months of unexplained anxiety. The young man who got his energy back after his first ozone course. The grandfather who, on his last visit, simply said thank you.
